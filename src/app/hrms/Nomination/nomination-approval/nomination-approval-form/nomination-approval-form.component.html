1. 125/DDB/254 8/8/2019 Construction BSHB Document Bihar State Housing Board (BSHB) established under Bihar State Housing Board Act 1962 as a successor to Mysore Housing Board constituted in 1956. The primary objective of BSHB is 'to make such schemes and to carry out such works as are necessary for the purpose of dealing with and satisfying the need of housing accommodation'. With this directive BSHB endeavors to provide housing to the people of Bihar at affordable cost and therefore recognized as the most important agency for housing throughout Bihar.
